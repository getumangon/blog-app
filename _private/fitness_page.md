## Workout Plan

> Focus on form over weight — progress comes from patience.

---

## Day 1 — Chest & Triceps

**Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes of light cardio

| Exercise              | Sets | Reps       | Notes                          |
|-----------------------|------|------------|--------------------------------|
| Bench Press           | 3    | 8–10       | Focus on proper form           |
| Incline Barbell Press| 3    | 10         | Control the descent            |
| Chest Flyes           | 3    | 10–12      | Machine or dumbbells           |
| Tricep Dips           | 3    | Max reps   | Parallel bars or bench         |
| Tricep Pushdowns      | 3    | 12–15      | Cable machine                  |

**Cool Down:** Chest and tricep stretches — hold each for 20–30 seconds.

---

## Day 2 — Back & Biceps

**Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes of light cardio

| Exercise        | Sets | Reps    | Notes                    |
|-----------------|------|---------|--------------------------|
| Lat Pulldown    | 3    | 10      | Wide grip                |
| Seated Row      | 3    | 10      | Cable machine            |
| Dumbbell Row    | 3    | 10–12   | Per arm, keep back flat  |
| Bicep Curls     | 3    | 12      | Dumbbells or barbell     |
| Hammer Curls    | 3    | 12      | Neutral grip             |

**Cool Down:** Back and bicep stretches — hold each for 20–30 seconds.

---

## Day 3 — Shoulders & Abs

**Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes of light cardio

| Exercise                | Sets | Reps       | Notes              |
|-------------------------|------|------------|--------------------|
| Overhead Shoulder Press | 3    | 10         | Dumbbells or machine |
| Lateral Raises          | 3    | 12         | Slow and controlled |
| Front Raises            | 3    | 12         | Alternate arms     |
| Plank                   | 3    | 30–60 sec  | Keep hips level    |
| Russian Twists          | 3    | 20         | Add weight as needed |
| Leg Raises              | 3    | 12–15      | Lower back pressed flat |

**Cool Down:** Shoulder and core stretches — hold each for 20–30 seconds.

---

## Day 4 — Full Body Circuit

**Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes of light cardio

Complete **3 rounds** of the following circuit. Rest **1–2 minutes** between rounds.

| Exercise        | Reps       | Notes              |
|-----------------|------------|--------------------|
| Goblet Squats   | 15         | Hold a dumbbell    |
| Push-Ups        | 12–15      | Full range of motion |
| Dumbbell Rows   | 12 per arm | Keep back flat     |
| Lunges          | 12 per leg | Step through fully |
| Bicep Curls     | 15         | Control the movement |
| Tricep Dips     | 12–15      | Bench or parallel bars |

**Cool Down:** Full-body stretching sequence.

---

## Day 5 — Legs

**Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes of light cardio

| Exercise       | Sets | Reps  | Notes                                    |
|----------------|------|-------|------------------------------------------|
| Squats         | 3    | 10    | Bodyweight first, progress to barbell    |
| Leg Press      | 3    | 10–12 | Feet shoulder-width apart               |
| Leg Curl       | 3    | 12    | Machine, controlled movement             |
| Leg Extension  | 3    | 12    | Machine, squeeze at the top              |
| Calf Raises    | 4    | 15    | Machine or bodyweight, full stretch      |

**Cool Down:** Quad, hamstring, and calf stretches.

---

## Day 6 — Active Recovery or Cardio

Choose one option based on how your body feels:

### Option 1 — Light Cardio
- 30–45 minutes of **steady-state cardio**
- Brisk walking, cycling, or swimming at a comfortable pace

### Option 2 — Active Recovery
- Yoga or guided stretching session
- Leisurely walk outdoors
- Foam rolling tight muscle groups

> **Tip:** Active recovery is just as important as training days. Don't skip it.

---

## Day 7 — Rest

Take a full day off. Your muscles grow during recovery, not during the workout.

- Stay **hydrated** — aim for 2–3 litres of water
- Prioritise **sleep** — 7–9 hours
- Eat enough **protein** to support muscle repair
- Reflect on the week and plan adjustments for next week
